# Autogrow ERP Suite

Standalone Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) console for Autogrow commercial CEA hardware operations, decoupled from storefront & SCADA repositories until integration phase.

## Modules
- **Executive Overview**: Consolidated revenue, fulfillment pipeline, stock alerts, and monthly cashflow analytics by business entity.
- **Sales Orders & Fulfillment**: Web checkout, commercial B2B quote, and distributor orders with stock allocation, dispatch tracking, and invoice issuance.
- **Inventory & Warehouse**: Hardware controllers, sensory pods, dosing skids, and spare parts tracked by warehouse bin, reserved stock, and reorder points.
- **Procurement & Suppliers**: Purchase orders with goods-receiving workflow that automatically replenishes warehouse inventory.
- **Finance & Invoices**: Accounts receivable ledger tied to fulfilled orders with payment reconciliation.

## Local Development

npm install
